Frequently Asked Questions

What is AI?

Artificial intelligence refers to computer systems that can perform tasks normally requiring human intelligence, such as understanding language and solving problems.

Can beginners learn AI?

Yes. Many modern AI tools are designed for non-technical users and require no programming knowledge.

Are AI tools free?

Many tools offer free plans, although advanced features may require a paid subscription.

Which AI tool should I start with?

ChatGPT is one of the easiest tools for beginners, because it can help with learning, writing and research.
