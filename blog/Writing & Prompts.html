Writing & Prompts Center

Welcome to the Prompt Engineering resource center. Learning how to write effective prompts is the single most important skill when working with Generative AI tools. Here, we break down actionable writing frameworks.

The Act-As Framework

To get the best copywriting output, always assign a specific professional persona to the AI before asking it to write paragraphs or essays.

"Act as a professional copywriter with 10 years of SEO experience. Rewrite the following product description to make it highly engaging for young adults."

Iterative Prompting Secrets

Never expect a perfect response on your very first try. AI response flows are conversation-driven. If the initial output is too long, reply with "Shorten this by 50% and format as bullet points." This collaborative refinement technique drastically elevates final text asset quality.
