Understanding AI Fundamentals

Artificial Intelligence can seem incredibly complex from the outside, but understanding the core structural mechanics helps clear up the mystery for absolute beginners.

What is a Large Language Model (LLM)?

An LLM is essentially an advanced statistical math machine designed to predict the next logical word in a sequence. Trained on vast libraries of human literature, these networks recognize deep patterns in linguistics, allowing them to simulate conversation.

The Difference Between Training and Inference

Training is the massive computational phase where a neural network learns patterns from initial raw data. Inference is the live phase where a user asks a finished model a question, and it calculates a helpful text response instantly.
