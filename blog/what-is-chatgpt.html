What is ChatGPT? (And How to Use It Effectively)

ChatGPT is an AI tool that helps you write text, answer questions, generate ideas, and solve problems. It works like a smart assistant you can talk to.

What Can You Use ChatGPT For?

  • Writing blog posts and articles
  • Summarizing long text
  • Generating ideas for content or business
  • Helping with coding and debugging

Simple Example

Instead of searching Google for hours, you can ask:

Explain blockchain in simple words with examples.

ChatGPT will give you a clear explanation in seconds.

Limitations

  • Sometimes gives incorrect information
  • Needs clear prompts to work well

Conclusion

ChatGPT is not just a chatbot. It is a productivity tool that can save time in learning, writing, and working.
